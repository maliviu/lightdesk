LightDesk — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

LightDesk is an app for controlling Insteon lights through a Universal Devices ISY994i controller on your home network. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Summary

We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send anything to us or to any third party.

What the app stores on your device

  • Connection settings: the controller’s address, port and username, and your choices such as the refresh interval. These are kept in the app’s preferences on your device.
  • Controller password: kept in the iOS Keychain on your device, only if “Remember password” is switched on. It is not included in backups to other devices.
  • Your list of lights and scenes: which of the controller’s lights and scenes you added to the Lights screen.
  • Activity log: the commands sent and the controller’s replies, kept in memory only while the app is open. It never contains your password.

Deleting the app removes its settings. To remove the saved password without deleting the app, switch off “Remember password” and connect again.

Network connections

The app connects only to the controller address you enter, on your local network, to read the list and status of your lights and to send the commands you tap. Your username and password are sent to that controller to sign in. If you use plain HTTP, they travel unencrypted over your Wi-Fi, so use it only on a network you trust. The app makes no other connections.

Permissions

Local Network: iOS asks for this the first time the app connects to your controller. It is used only to reach the controller.

Sharing

Nothing is shared by the app itself. If you choose to share the activity log with the iOS share sheet, it goes only where you send it.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
